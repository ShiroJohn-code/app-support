ShiftAlarm 隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 11 月 16 日

ShiftAlarm 是一款協助輪班工作者管理班表、鬧鐘提醒、請假與加班紀錄的 iOS App。 我們重視你的隱私。本政策說明 ShiftAlarm 如何處理你的資料。

我們儲存的資料

ShiftAlarm 可能會在你的裝置上儲存以下資料：

  • 班別名稱、班別時間、班別顏色與鬧鐘設定
  • 輪班規則與產生的班表資料
  • 手動修改的班表、換班、請假、加班與備註
  • App 設定，例如主題、介面大小、工作地時區、通知偏好與 Widget 顯示資料
  • 鬧鐘識別碼、預定時間、聲音偏好、略過紀錄與系統讀回狀態，用於管理提醒
  • 購買狀態，用於判斷是否已解鎖進階鬧鐘功能

資料儲存位置

ShiftAlarm 的資料主要儲存在你的裝置本機。若你已登入 iCloud 並啟用系統支援， 班別、輪班規則與手動班表異動可能會透過你的私人 iCloud 進行同步。

Widget 所需的今日與明日班表資訊會透過 App Group 儲存在裝置上，讓主 App 與 Widget 可以共享必要資料。 捷徑使用的班表快照與鬧鐘管理狀態也會儲存在裝置上；工作地時區設定可能透過 iCloud 同步。 工作地時區由你選擇，App 不會為此取得 GPS 定位。

我們不會做的事

  • 我們不會販售你的個人資料。
  • 我們不會使用你的資料進行廣告追蹤。
  • 我們不會使用第三方分析 SDK 追蹤你的使用行為。
  • 我們不會將你的班表資料上傳到開發者自行營運的伺服器。

鬧鐘與通知權限

在支援 AlarmKit 的系統上，ShiftAlarm 會在你選擇啟用系統鬧鐘時請求鬧鐘權限， 讓 iOS 管理你設定的工作鬧鐘。鬧鐘狀態可能顯示於鎖定畫面或動態島。 你可以在系統設定管理鬧鐘權限；較舊系統可使用需自行設定的捷徑流程。

ShiftAlarm 會在你同意後使用通知權限，為你的輪班與鬧鐘設定提供本地提醒。 你可以隨時在 iOS 系統設定中關閉通知權限。

行事曆權限

ShiftAlarm 會在你主動選擇匯出班表時請求行事曆權限。 此權限僅用於將你的班表新增或更新到 iOS 行事曆中。

iCloud 同步

若使用 iCloud 同步，相關資料會儲存在你的私人 iCloud 帳戶中。 iCloud 的資料處理由 Apple 的 iCloud 服務條款與隱私政策管理。

App 內購買

ShiftAlarm 使用 Apple 的 App Store 內購買系統處理進階功能解鎖。 購買與付款資訊由 Apple 處理，ShiftAlarm 不會取得你的信用卡或付款資料。

資料匯出與刪除

你可以在 App 內匯出或匯入備份資料，也可以主動分享月曆圖片或匯出鬧鐘診斷 JSON。 備份與月曆圖片可能包含班表及備註；診斷檔可能包含班別名稱、鬧鐘時間、識別碼、系統版本與時區。 這些檔案不會自動傳送給開發者；只有你透過系統分享功能選定的 App 或收件者會收到內容。 匯出的檔案請由你自行保管或刪除，鬧鐘聲音偏好不包含於班表備份。

你可以在 App 設定中清除班別、輪班規則與班表資料。若啟用 iCloud，同步刪除也可能傳至其他裝置。 系統行事曆中已匯出的事件與你另存或分享的檔案，需要分別管理。

支援聯絡

若你主動寄送支援信件，開發者會收到你提供的電子郵件地址、信件內容與附件， 並用於回覆及排查問題。寄送前請檢查並移除不希望分享的班表或個人資訊。

兒童隱私

ShiftAlarm 並非專為兒童設計，也不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

政策變更

我們可能會不定期更新本隱私權政策。更新後的版本會發布於本頁面。

ShiftAlarm Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

ShiftAlarm is an iOS app designed to help shift workers manage rotating schedules, alarm reminders, leave records, overtime records, and related notes. We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ShiftAlarm handles your data.

Data Stored by the App

ShiftAlarm may store the following data on your device:

  • Shift names, shift times, shift colors, and alarm settings
  • Rotation rules and generated schedule entries
  • Manually edited schedules, shift swaps, leave records, overtime records, and notes
  • App settings, such as theme, interface size, work time zone, notification preferences, and Widget display data
  • Alarm identifiers, scheduled times, sound preferences, skip records, and system-reported states used to manage reminders
  • Purchase status used to determine whether advanced alarm features have been unlocked

Where Your Data Is Stored

ShiftAlarm stores your data primarily on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud and system support is available, your shift types, rotation rules, and manual schedule changes may be synced through your private iCloud account.

Today and tomorrow shift information required by the Widget may be stored locally through an App Group so the main app and the Widget can share the necessary data. Shortcut schedule snapshots and alarm management records are also stored locally. Your selected work time zone may sync through iCloud. Selecting a work time zone does not use GPS location.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising tracking.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs to track your behavior.
  • We do not upload your schedule data to developer-operated servers.

Alarms and Notifications

On systems that support AlarmKit, ShiftAlarm requests alarm permission when you choose to enable system alarms, allowing iOS to manage the work alarms you configure. Alarm states may appear on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island. You can manage alarm permission in system settings. Older systems can use a Shortcuts workflow that requires manual setup.

With your permission, ShiftAlarm uses notifications to provide local reminders for your shifts and alarm settings. You can disable notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Calendar Access

ShiftAlarm requests calendar access only when you choose to export your schedule. This permission is used only to add or update your schedule events in the iOS Calendar app.

iCloud Sync

If iCloud sync is used, related data is stored in your private iCloud account. iCloud data processing is governed by Apple’s iCloud terms and privacy policy.

In-App Purchases

ShiftAlarm uses Apple’s App Store in-app purchase system to unlock advanced features. Purchase and payment information is processed by Apple. ShiftAlarm does not receive your credit card or payment details.

Data Export and Deletion

You can export or import backups, share a calendar image, or export an alarm diagnostic JSON file. Backups and calendar images may contain schedules and notes. Diagnostic files may contain shift names, alarm times, identifiers, system version, and time zone. Files are not automatically sent to the developer; they are shared only with apps or recipients you choose in the system share sheet. You manage the storage and deletion of exported files. Schedule backups exclude alarm sound preferences.

You can clear shift types, rotation rules, and schedules in app settings. If iCloud sync is enabled, those deletions may sync to other devices. Exported calendar events and separately saved or shared files must be managed separately.

Support Requests

If you choose to email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and attachments you provide and uses them to respond and troubleshoot. Review attachments and remove any schedule or personal information you do not wish to share before sending them.

Children’s Privacy

ShiftAlarm is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page.
