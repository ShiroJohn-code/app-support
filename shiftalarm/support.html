常見問題

如何啟用鬧鐘？

先在「班表」建立班別與輪班規則，再到「鬧鐘」的「鬧鐘設定」選擇提醒方式。 iOS 26 以上可在授權後使用 AlarmKit 系統鬧鐘；較舊系統使用需要手動安裝與設定的捷徑流程。 以 App 顯示的系統確認數量及覆蓋日期核對排程，定期開啟 App 更新。 背景刷新由 iOS 決定執行時間，無法保證每天固定時間續排。

鬧鐘過了預定時間仍顯示待處理？

「鬧鐘」總覽的「仍待處理的鬧鐘」會列出可確認的保留項目。 使用該項目的停止入口後，App 會重新讀取系統狀態；若仍無法確認，請保留畫面並重試。 原定時間已過不代表鬧鐘已停止，稍後提醒也可能仍在作用中。

換裝置後聲音設定不同？

AlarmKit 聲音偏好由各裝置分別保存，不包含於班表備份。 匯入或在其他裝置還原後，請在鬧鐘聲音設定重新選擇；未設定時使用系統預設聲音。

如何提供鬧鐘診斷？

可在鬧鐘設定匯出診斷 JSON，再自行決定是否寄至支援信箱。 檔案可能包含班別名稱、鬧鐘時間、識別碼、系統版本與時區，寄送前請先檢查內容。 匯出不會自動傳送，也不會變更既有鬧鐘。

通知沒有出現怎麼辦？

請先確認 iOS 系統設定中已允許 ShiftAlarm 傳送通知，並確認 App 內的備援通知功能已開啟。

行事曆匯出失敗怎麼辦？

請確認你已授予 ShiftAlarm 行事曆完整存取權限，並重新嘗試匯出。

iCloud 同步沒有立即更新怎麼辦？

iCloud 同步由系統管理，可能需要一些時間。請確認裝置已登入 iCloud，且網路連線正常。

購買後沒有解鎖怎麼辦？

請在 App 內使用「恢復購買」功能。如果仍無法解鎖，請透過支援信箱聯絡我們。

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enable alarms?

Create shift types and a rotation rule in the Schedule tab, then choose a delivery method in Alarm Settings. On iOS 26 or later, you can authorize AlarmKit system alarms. Older systems use a Shortcuts workflow that requires manual installation and setup. Check the system-confirmed count and coverage dates in the app, and open the app regularly to refresh coverage. iOS controls background refresh timing; daily refresh at a fixed time is not guaranteed.

Why does an alarm remain after its scheduled time?

The alarm overview lists identifiable retained items under alarms requiring attention. Use an item's stop control and let the app read the system state again. If removal cannot be confirmed, keep the status visible and retry. A past scheduled time does not prove an alarm has stopped; a snoozed alarm may still be active.

Why are sound settings different on another device?

AlarmKit sound preferences are stored separately on each device and are excluded from schedule backups. After importing or restoring on another device, choose sounds again in alarm sound settings. Unconfigured alarms use the system default sound.

How can I share alarm diagnostics?

Export a diagnostic JSON file from Alarm Settings and choose whether to email it to support. It may contain shift names, alarm times, identifiers, system version, and time zone; review it before sharing. Exporting does not automatically send the file or change existing alarms.

What should I do if notifications do not appear?

Please check that notifications are allowed for ShiftAlarm in iOS Settings, and make sure backup notifications are enabled in the app.

What should I do if calendar export fails?

Please confirm that ShiftAlarm has full calendar access permission, then try exporting again.

What should I do if iCloud sync does not update immediately?

iCloud sync is managed by the system and may take some time. Please make sure your device is signed in to iCloud and has a working network connection.

What should I do if my purchase is not unlocked?

Please use the “Restore Purchases” feature in the app. If the issue continues, contact us by email.
